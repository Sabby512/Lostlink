What is LostLink?

The UCLM campus lost and found system that links lost reports to found items.

  • 1
    Zone-based location matchingItems found in your campus zone are ranked first.
  • 2
    Smart match scoringName, category, location and date build a match score.
  • 3
    Admin-reviewed returnsEvery claim is checked against your proof of ownership.
  • 4
    Instant match alertsGet a bell and email alert when a likely match is found.
  • 5
    Private by designSensitive item details stay hidden until you are verified.
